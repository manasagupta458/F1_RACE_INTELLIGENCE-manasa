Phase 5: Performance Testing

1. Objective

To evaluate the Formula 1 Race Intelligence Dashboard in terms of performance, interactivity, and visualization quality.

2. Testing Parameters

Amount of Data Rendered

Review the amount of Formula 1 data displayed in the dashboard and verify that the visualizations present the intended information correctly.

Utilization of Filters

Check the dashboard filters to ensure that they work correctly and update the relevant visualizations.

Number of Calculation Fields

Review the calculated fields used in Tableau and verify that their results are correct.

Number of Visualizations

Count the charts and graphs included in the dashboard and check whether they communicate the intended information clearly.

Dashboard Performance

Test dashboard loading, filter responsiveness, and the display of visualizations.

3. Expected Outcome

The dashboard should display its visualizations correctly, respond appropriately to user interactions, and provide a smooth data exploration experience.

4. Testing Results

Record the actual number of filters, calculated fields, visualizations, and observations after testing the Tableau workbook.
