About the Project

The Formula 1 Race Intelligence Dashboard is a data visualization project designed to explore Formula 1 racing information through interactive charts and graphs. It helps users understand circuit information, race performance, driver comparisons, team performance, and pit stop analysis.

Project Features

🏁 Circuit Analysis

Explore Formula 1 circuits and compare circuit-related information.

📊 Race Performance

Understand race trends and compare performance across races.

🏎️ Driver Analysis

Explore driver performance and compare available driver statistics.

🏆 Team Performance

Compare Formula 1 teams using the information presented in the dashboard.

⏱️ Pit Stop Analysis

Explore pit stop information and compare available pit stop data.
